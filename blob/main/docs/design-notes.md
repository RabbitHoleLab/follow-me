# FOLLOW ME — DESIGN NOTES

**Project:** FOLLOW ME  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Reference:** RHL-DN-001

## Overview

FOLLOW ME is an interactive experiment exploring how individual perspectives influence the interpretation of a shared environment.

The experience introduces four characters, each approaching the same situation with different expectations and assumptions.

## Interaction Principles

The experiment is built around three principles:

- **Perspective:** What a player notices depends partly on where they stand.
- **Interpretation:** The same event may carry different meanings for different observers.
- **Decision-making:** The process of selecting one possibility from several available alternatives. (12)

The experience does not attempt to identify a universally correct path.

## Design Considerations

Player actions should feel meaningful without requiring a conventional success or failure condition.

Repeated interactions may reveal additional narrative elements, encouraging players to reconsider earlier assumptions.

## Internal Notes

The relationship between perception, interpretation and decision-making remains central to future development.
